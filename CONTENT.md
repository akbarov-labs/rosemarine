## Brand

- **Name:** ROSEMARINE (Rosemarine Organic)
- **Tagline:** "Tabiatdan bir bo'lak 🌱" — *a piece of nature*
- **Pillars:** ORGANIC • BENEFICIAL • HANDMADE
- **Founder:** "Organik baxtlar MEHRCHISI (BIOLOG)" — a biologist; products are lab-tested ("laboratoriya tekshiruvidan o'tgan")
- **Proof:** 3,000+ satisfied customers; ~8.6K Instagram, ~3.6K Telegram followers; takes part in the "Yashil Uyim" eco festivals (Tashkent, Andijan, Namangan)
- **Eco packaging:** soaps in cloth pouches, instructions on paper, liquids in PET, cream & balm in sterilised glass jars, kraft-paper outer packaging
- **Longevity:** products last at least 2 months
- **Colors seen in brand assets:** deep forest green (logo/labels), warm cream/peach background, kraft brown, soft pink accents
- **Logo:** line-drawn tulip/sprout over a wave + serif "ROSEMARINE" (`assets/images/brand-logo.jpg`) — redraw as SVG for the site

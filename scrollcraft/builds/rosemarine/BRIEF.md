# Rosemarine Organic: BRIEF

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user said: "I guess web site should be some kind of artistic … their photo and all content made by heart, they know inspiring people, now I am gonna inspire them, but I don't know how to do it to be honest, let's discuss, you are the expert on it", then: "can we make something beautiful with scroll-craft". Languages were decided by the user: "3 language: Uzbek, Russian and English".

Evidence comes from the brand's public Telegram channel (1,248 posts) and its Instagram profile. Everything else is labeled as an authored decision.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (authored):** tender, handmade, scientific-warm, unhurried. References: a 19th-century herbarium sheet with its stitched specimen and lower-right label; Agnes Varda's *The Gleaners and I* (gathering by hand); the brand's own kraft pouches and dark-green labels.
2. **Journey (authored, built from the brand's own content order):** a title page drawn by hand; a note from the founder (a biologist); what is not in their products; one plant becoming one soap; the collection; "Muammo → Nima qilaman?" (the brand's own format); customer notes; gifts ("the attention, not the price"); the order slip sent to @rmehrchi.
3. **Energy curve (authored):** quiet open, a slow build, a hush, the loudest moment at the rose, abundance, then a calmer, practical close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see the feeling curve and the peak below.
5. **What no other site does (authored, from the logo):** the logo is drawn as one continuous line. On this site that line becomes a green thread. It draws the logo, crosses out the bad ingredients, draws a rose that turns into the real soap, holds the specimens up like a drying line, and finally ties itself back into the logo on your order slip.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal (authored):** editorial and handmade. Paper, tape, thread and labels. It is neither luxury-dark nor maximalist.
7. **Unbroken world or scenes (authored):** distinct sheets. A herbarium is a stack of separate pages, so each act is its own sheet with hard edges, joined only by the thread.
8. **Assets:** 47 curated brand photos from Telegram (max ~800px), the logo (raster, to be redrawn as SVG) and real captions, prices and reviews. No image-generation key is set and none is needed: the photos are mounted as small prints on paper, which suits their resolution.

## Grammar: Herbarium folio (new)

- **Unit:** a mounted sheet. Every act is a paper sheet or a page of the cabinet. Photos are prints taped onto paper, never full-bleed.
- **Nav:** a thumb index (tabs on the right edge, like a field book) that jumps between sheets, plus a language switch and the order-slip count. On phones it collapses to a top strip with an index drawer.
- **Sequence:** title sheet → letter → specimens → collection → order slip.
- **Ending:** a herbarium-style label (the order slip) that the visitor fills in by choosing products, then sends.
- **Bans:** full-bleed photography, scrims (nothing is laid over a photo), `scrub` video, character-level kinetic type, `magnet`, `spotlight`, colour `drift` (sheets are hard cuts on the cabinet ground), centred hero copy.

**Why the other grammars lost:**
- Filmic one-shot: there is no footage, and its seamlessness would erase the sheet metaphor.
- Chaptered editorial: closest, but it bans media in the hero, and the hero needs the depth of the stacked sheets.
- Live surface: this isn't software.
- Continuous world: there is no geography to travel through.
- Typographic poster: the brand's photos and drawings carry the story more than any sentence does.
- Gallery: too cold for a founder story.
- Split stage: there is no two-sided argument.
- Cutlist: too fast for the "made with mehr" tone.

## Honesty rules for this build

- Prices are the last plain-text prices the brand posted (June to August 2026). Recent posts hide the digits, so the owner must confirm them before launch. Totals are labeled approximate.
- Reviews are quoted from the brand's own reposts, with first name and initial only. On the RU and EN pages they are translated and marked as such.
- No counters. "3000+" is the brand's own claim and is shown as static text.

## Revision 2026-10-02
Act 9 is no longer pinned. On viewports wider than 860px but short (landscape phones, tablets, phones in desktop mode) the pinned frame clipped the order form, and the user could not scroll to it. The order form must never depend on scroll position, so it is now a plain section everywhere. Its cues were removed, and the thread still redraws the logo when the slip comes into view.
